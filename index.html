Chat server (needed for YouTube + TikTok)

YouTube and TikTok chat comes through your own free chat server. Set it up once:

  1. Click Deploy to Render and sign in with GitHub.
  2. Click Deploy Blueprint (or Apply) and wait for it to say Live. That takes about 3 minutes.
  3. Copy your server's address (it looks like https://lethal-chat-server-xxxx.onrender.com) and paste it below.
